About WOK!N

Modern Pan-Asian Kitchen · Gulberg Greens, Islamabad

WOK!N is a modern pan-Asian kitchen serving hand-tossed favourites from across China, Thailand, and beyond. Everything is cooked fresh to order in a screaming-hot wok, then packed and sent straight to your door — or ready for pick-up from our Gulberg Greens branch.

What we're about

  • Fresh, never frozen — dishes are prepared when you order, not before.
  • Bold pan-Asian flavour — from Kung Pao and Manchurian to chow mein, fried rice, dynamite prawns, and warm soups.
  • Fast & friendly — delivery in around 45 minutes, pick-up in around 20.
