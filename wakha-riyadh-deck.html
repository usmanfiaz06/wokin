A 200-YEAR TRADITION

Meat first.
Spice second.

Shinwari cuisine comes from the Pashtun tribes of the Khyber Pass — a tradition built on premium meat, live fire, and spice used to enhance, never to mask.

No marinades. No sauces. Just craft.

THE HERO PLATE THREE DISHES · ONE TRADITION SLIDE 06 / 16

Three dishes
tell the whole story.

Every Shinwari house is judged on these three — and Wakha is built around them.
ONE · THE HERO
نمكين

Namkeen Gosht

SALT-ROAST LAMB

The Shinwari signature. Lamb cooked over open flame with only salt and pepper. A 200-year tribal recipe — the dish a Shinwari restaurant is judged on.

TWO · THE FAVOURITE
چپلی

Chapli Kabab

FIRE-SEARED MINCE

Hand-pounded, spiced and seared on a flat griddle. The most beloved street-to-fine-dining crossover dish in the Pashtun region.

THREE · THE THEATRE
كڑاہی

Karahi

LIVE-WOK FIRE

Lamb or chicken finished in a roaring iron wok, brought to the table still bubbling. The most photographed dish in modern Pashtun dining.
